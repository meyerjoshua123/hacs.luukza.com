🌡️ HVAC Simulators · v0.3.0

Know what your HVAC is doing,
without plugs on it.

HVAC Simulators watches how each room's temperature, humidity and CO2 change. It takes away what the weather, the building and your plugged-in appliances explain, and attributes the rest to your aircon, heater, fans or open windows. You get simulated appliances with modes, states, setpoints and energy, and they get more accurate every time you correct them.

How it works

From sensor wiggles to "the aircon is cooling"

Every minute (or every sensor update), for every room:

The rules come from physics: heat flows towards the colder side, CO2 only clears through real air exchange, and an aircon's coil pulls moisture out while it runs. What you teach is stored as examples. The more you teach, the more weight your examples get over the rules.

What it detects

A cause for every change

🔥

Heating

Warming faster than the building shell explains, with a heater or heat-pump appliance to credit.

❄️

Cooling

Temperature dropping fast while humidity lags, or a regular compressor cycle showing in humidity.

💧

Dehumidify & humidify

Humidity moving hard while the temperature barely moves, ignoring rises a shower in the same zone explains.

🌀

Extractor fans & ventilation

CO2 clearing faster than natural leakage while the doors and windows are known to be closed.

🫧

Air filtering

Particulates improving while CO2 isn't. Filters clean air but can't remove CO2.

🚪

Open doors & windows

From door/window sensors, or estimated from two signs of outside air getting in.

Solar gain

Warming with the sun up, when it's warm outside or the room is already well above outdoor on a mild day.

🧱

Drift towards the outdoor temperature at the rate your building's shell allows.

🔌

Appliance heat & moisture

A fridge, dryer, oven or stove explaining the change, learned from its smart plug.

Rooms & zones

Your home, room by room

Every Home Assistant area can become a room with its own sensors, air volume and known air gaps. Link the rooms that flow into each other (an open doorway, a hallway, a gap under a door) and they form a zone.

  • A bathroom fan in a bathroom with no sensors is seen through the CO2 in the living room it connects to.
  • A shower is detected from the bathroom's humidity surge, and stops the rest of the zone blaming a humidifier.
  • Put the dryer's plug in the laundry, and its heat and moisture are learned there.
  • Room-vs-room sensors show things like the laundry versus the living room.
Cycling

The cycling you can't see

In an apartment, an aircon can run all night while the temperature barely moves, because the building's stored heat flows back in as fast as the unit removes it. The compressor still leaves a fingerprint: humidity falls while it runs and recovers while it rests, every 15–25 minutes.

HVAC Simulators finds that rhythm and ignores random wander. The unit stays in cool mode while its state follows the compressor between active and idle. Energy is charged for the real on-time, and it learns how far your room sensor reads from the unit's own thermostat.

Appliances

Simulated appliances with real states

HVAC units by function

Tick Heat, Ventilate and/or Air condition and get the matching modes: heat, cool, dry, fresh-air ventilate and indoor filter. Set power per mode, a setpoint range and whether it's an inverter.

Mode, state & setpoint

Each appliance has a mode, a state (active, idle or off) and a reason it's idle. The setpoint is detected from where the room turns around.

Manual control

Tell it the mode and setpoint you set. You can have that revert to auto after a set time, and it spots when the unit was actually switched off.

Extractor fan groups

Several fans as one group. It estimates how many are on from CO2, and once you calibrate each fan, which ones.

Plugged-in appliances

Fridge, washer, dryer, oven, stove or general appliances get an active/idle/off status from their plug, plus cycles, duty cycle and run length. Their heat and moisture is removed before detection.

Dehumidifiers, humidifiers & purifiers

Standalone appliances that the matching cause can be attributed to.

Insight

Numbers you can act on

⚡

Power & energy

Estimated watts per appliance, including fan-only and idle power, plus a kWh sensor for the Energy dashboard. Or feed the power into PowerCalc.

🧯

Effectiveness & filters

Heating and cooling performance compared with just after the last filter clean, at the same outdoor conditions. Rated Excellent to Terrible, with notifications.

🏡

Efficiency record

Each room's heat-loss coefficient, heat lost, solar gain, heat leaked in and air changes, day by day. Compare seasons, or your old and new home.

👥

People from CO2

How many people are in a zone, adjusted for sleeping, resting, active or exercising, and calibrated with a head count.

📈

Differences & rates

Indoor versus outdoor temperature and absolute humidity (should you open a window?), and rates since the last reading and over 5, 10 and 15 minutes.

🎓

Learning

Confirm or correct suggestions, calibrate setpoints, occupancy and fans. It also learns each room's heat-exchange rate and which appliance usually does what.
