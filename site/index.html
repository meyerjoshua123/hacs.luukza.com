The integrations

Pick what you need

Each one stands on its own. Both install the same way through HACS.

🌡️

HVAC Simulators

v0.3.0 · active development

Works out what your aircon, heater, fans and open windows are doing from how each room's temperature, humidity and CO2 change, without smart plugs on them. It learns from you as you confirm or correct it.

  • Heating, cooling, dehumidifying, ventilation, solar gain, heat loss…
  • Rooms from your areas, and zones from how they connect
  • Compressor cycling, setpoints, effectiveness and filter reminders
  • Power and energy, people from CO2, and a building efficiency record

📦

History Archiver

v0.1.0 · early

Continuously samples your numeric entities into its own SQLite database, together with device metadata, so you have long-term history ready for analysis, independent of Home Assistant's recorder.

  • Configurable sampling interval (default 10 s)
  • Device and entity metadata alongside every entity
  • Fast SQLite storage with WAL and schema versioning
  • Export engine for CSV, JSON, XLSX, Parquet and more (being wired up)

Getting started

Install in a couple of minutes

1️⃣

Have HACS

HACS is the Home Assistant Community Store. If you don't have it yet, follow the HACS setup guide.

2️⃣

Add the repository

HACS → ⋮ → Custom repositories, paste the GitHub link, and choose the Integration category.

3️⃣

Download & add

Download it, restart Home Assistant, then go to Settings → Devices & services → Add integration.

🔒

Local-first

Everything runs inside your Home Assistant. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

🧪

Tested

HVAC Simulators ships with a test suite that runs inside a real Home Assistant core, including a replay of a real overnight recording.
