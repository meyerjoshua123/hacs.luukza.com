📦 History Archiver · v0.1.0 (early)

Your entity history,
kept and ready to analyse.

History Archiver samples your numeric entities at a steady interval into its own SQLite database, together with each device's details. That gives you clean, regular long-term data you can analyse or export, independent of how Home Assistant's recorder is set up.

Install GitHub

Early release. In v0.1.0, recording works: entities are sampled into the database with their metadata. The export engine, profiles, scheduled and predefined exports, and backup/restore are all in the code but not yet exposed as services or settings. The status table shows exactly what's available today.

⏱️

Continuous sampling

Records every numeric entity every few seconds (default 10 s), so the data is evenly spaced and easy to analyse. Choosing entities is coming.

🗄️

Its own SQLite database

Stored at /config/history_archiver/history.db, with WAL mode, async I/O and schema versioning for safe upgrades.

🏷️

Device metadata

Keeps manufacturer, model, software and hardware version, device class, entity category, integration, area, device and entity name alongside your data.

📤

Multi-format export engine

CSV, JSON, HTML, XLSX, SQLite, Parquet, Feather and Arrow. coming

📉

Downsampling

Export raw samples, or a mean or time-weighted mean per interval. coming

🗂️

Profiles & schedules

Named sets of entities with their own formats and schedules, plus day, week, month and year exports. coming

💾

Backup & restore

Timestamped copies of the database, for migrating or recovering. coming

🔒

Local-first

No cloud and no external services. Your history stays in your Home Assistant config folder.

🔗

Pairs with HVAC Simulators

Archive the room trends, appliance states and efficiency sensors to study your home over months.

Get started

Install through HACS and pick a sampling interval. The help page covers what's stored, how much space it takes, and how to query it.
